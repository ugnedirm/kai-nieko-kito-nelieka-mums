Welcome to When nothing else is left

When nothing else is left saves a summary of your ChatGPT or Claude chat on your own computer, so you can continue the same work in another AI, or later, without starting over. Nothing is sent to us or anywhere else.

Step 1. Start your vault (once)

The vault is a small program on your computer that keeps your saved chats. Open the Terminal app (press Cmd+Space, type Terminal, press Enter), paste this line, press Enter and answer Yes:

thread setup

It creates your vault and makes it start by itself each time you log in, so you only do this once. (If you do not have it yet, install it first: see the README.)

Step 3. Save a chat

  1. Open a chat on ChatGPT or Claude.
  2. Click the When nothing else is left icon in the toolbar (the puzzle piece if you do not see it), then Save this chat's summary.
  3. Press send in the chat. The AI writes a short summary.
  4. A review page opens. Read it, fix anything wrong, press Save.

Do not wait for the limit. By default you are reminded every 10 replies to save a summary, so one already exists if you run out of messages. Change this any time in the extension icon, under Checkpoints.

To read or reuse what you saved: click the icon, then Open my saved threads. Each thread has a Copy for another AI button: paste it into any other AI to carry on.

Good to know

  • You always review the summary before it is saved. If it contains something that looks like a password or key, the vault refuses to save it.
  • The AI's summary can be wrong. Treat it as notes, not facts.
